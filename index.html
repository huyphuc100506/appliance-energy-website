Average annual consumption

Mean labelled energy use of TVs with an internal power supply in the Australian Government dataset. External power supply models average about 100 kWh.

Energy Star ratings

More stars, less energy.

The star label compares a TV with others of similar screen size, so use it to pick between sets of the same size.

Financial benefit

About $139 vs $30 a year.

At 30 c/kWh, a 462 kWh TV costs around $139 to run each year. A 100 kWh set costs around $30.
