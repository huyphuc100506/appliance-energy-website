Decoding Television
Energy Efficiency

Four charts from the GEMS television dataset show how much power sets draw, and what separates the thrifty from the thirsty.

01 · The spread

Brand averages run from about 53 to 823 kWh a year.

Across roughly a hundred brands, the average climbs steadily, then jumps at the end. Most sit below 400 kWh, but the top few pass 600 kWh.

02 · The brands you know

Popular brands average roughly 290 to 600 kWh.

Household names cluster in the middle and upper range. Even the lowest popular brand average is more than three times the 90 kWh ceiling of the frugal top 10 below.

03 · Power supply type

External power supply models use about a quarter of the energy.

EPS models average near 100 kWh a year, against about 462 kWh for internal power supply sets. EPS units are often compact, so size likely plays a part.

04 · The top 10

The ten lowest brand averages all sit at or below 90 kWh.

ARK leads at about 53 kWh a year, with RCA the tenth at about 90. Values are read from the chart, so treat them as approximate.
