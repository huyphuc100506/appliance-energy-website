About the project

This site presents a data story on television energy use in Australia and a calculator that turns wattage into a yearly running cost.

It was created for COS30045 Data Visualization at Swinburne University of Technology. The unit is about turning data into clear, honest visuals, and this project applies that to the GEMS television dataset.

  • Analysis and charts: built in KNIME and exported as images.
  • Data story: four charts explain how energy use varies by brand and power supply.
  • Web delivery: a three-page site in HTML, CSS and JavaScript with an interactive calculator.

Audience

Australian households, appliance shoppers and cost- or environment-conscious consumers.

They prefer short summaries, transparent data and simple interactions on both desktop and mobile.

Ethics

  • Only aggregated product data is shown, with no personal information.
  • Calculator inputs stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
  • Costs are estimates based on the price and hours you enter.
